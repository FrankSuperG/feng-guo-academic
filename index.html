I am Feng Guo, an FWO Senior Postdoctoral Fellow and IEEE Senior Member, working jointly with VITO and UHasselt in Belgium. My research connects electrochemical modelling and parameter identification with robust state estimation and fault-tolerant battery management.

Research question How can we estimate battery states accurately, reliably, and efficiently when sensing is limited, model parameters are uncertain, and sensors can fail?

Research Interests

01 / MODELS

Control-oriented electrochemical battery modelling

SPM/P2D model reduction, grouped electrochemical parameters, thermal coupling, and fast parameter identification with BOLT.

02 / ESTIMATION

Battery state estimation and parameter identification

Robust SOC estimation, physics-guided Kalman filtering, observer design, and joint state-parameter estimation under uncertainty.

03 / SAFETY

Battery safety, fault diagnosis, and fault-tolerant control

Sensor-fault diagnosis and fault-tolerant SOC estimation for reliable battery operation, extending towards interpretable ageing diagnostics and safety- and lifetime-aware control.

04 / LEARNING

Physics-guided AI for battery management systems

Hybrid physics-learning models that preserve interpretability while improving robustness under real operating conditions.
